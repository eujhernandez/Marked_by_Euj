Care after your session

Aftercare matters.

Follow the specific instructions provided by your tattoo artist. Contact a qualified healthcare professional if you have concerns about healing.

Keep it clean

Wash your hands before touching the tattoo and follow your artist’s cleaning instructions.

Don’t pick

Allow healing skin to shed naturally; avoid scratching or picking.

Avoid soaking

Avoid swimming and long soaks while the tattoo is healing.

Protect it

Follow your artist’s advice on sun exposure, clothing, and aftercare products.
